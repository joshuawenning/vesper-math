# Vesper Math illustration direction

Use `public/art/elian-painterly.webp` as the visual anchor for future artwork.

- Thin watercolor and gouache on warm ivory paper; selective, irregular drawn contours.
- Broad shapes, muted pigment, soft or unfinished edges, and space left to interpretation.
- Faces and clothing described economically. Natural, thoughtful expressions; strange creatures can remain solemn or unsettling.
- Keep each world's palette and story concept distinct without changing the medium.
- Avoid polished digital concept art, photographic skin, elaborate embroidery, ornamental gold, cute exaggerated expressions, and dense all-over detail.
- Simplify the drawing itself rather than adding paper grain to a detailed rendering.

World landscapes and story scenes use landscape compositions. Leave the lower part of world landscapes quiet enough for the adventure title. Keep important story objects clear at thumbnail size. Portraits and reward studies use the same medium and restrained detail.

`ELIAN-PAINTERLY-ART-PROMPTS.json` and `CAMPAIGN-PAINTERLY-ART-PROMPTS.json` record the generation briefs. Production illustrations are optimized WebP files in `public/art/`; content registries select the active versions. Previous artwork remains available for recovery.
