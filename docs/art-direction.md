# Art direction

Vesper Math should feel like a quiet, evocative fantasy illustration found in an old book. Use the [Elian portrait](../public/art/elian-painterly.webp) as the visual anchor for every new painting.

## The painted language

- Thin watercolor and gouache on warm ivory paper, with selective, irregular pencil or pen contours.
- Broad shapes, muted pigment, uneven washes, soft or unfinished edges, and space left to interpretation.
- Economical faces, natural expressions, simple clothing, and thoughtful poses. Beauty comes from character and composition rather than glamour.
- A mix of women, men, creatures, magical objects, and landscapes. Strange figures may be solemn, uncanny, or unsettling.
- Distinct world colors within one shared medium. Let a shadow suggest a figure or an empty wash suggest distance.

Simplify the drawing itself. A grain filter over a highly rendered picture does not achieve this direction.

Avoid photographic skin, glossy digital concept art, intricate embroidery and gold filigree, uniformly sharp detail, sparkling particles, cute exaggerated faces, and cinematic lighting. Do not copy a reference card’s subject, frame, lettering, or an individual artist’s work.

## Composition in the game

Worlds and story scenes use landscape compositions. Keep landmarks readable in a wide crop and the lower area quiet enough for the adventure title. A story thumbnail should communicate one clear figure or object.

Elian’s training scenes use full figures in a portrait composition with room for the interaction. Her identity stays consistent: an adult woman with chestnut hair, a faded teal cloak, an ivory tunic, and a calm, attentive presence.

Player portraits retain the six established identities, colors, and atlas positions. Reward cards are isolated object studies on ivory paper, with generous margins and recognizable silhouettes.

The interface complements the paintings: literary serif headings, restrained brass outlines, parchment surfaces, muted forest tones, and subtle world-specific paper colors. The circular star is the brand mark; adventure icons describe their own stories. Motion should be slow and optional through the system’s reduced-motion preference.

## Reusable art brief

> An original fantasy illustration in thin watercolor and gouache on warm ivory paper. Selective, irregular drawn contours; broad simple shapes; uneven translucent pigment; soft incomplete edges. Suggest more than you describe. Quiet, grown-up atmosphere, natural people, and solemn strange creatures. Muted color and restrained lighting. Preserve the supplied story concept, but remove unnecessary surface decoration. No photographic detail, glossy digital rendering, ornate embroidery, cute cartoon expressions, text, watermark, or decorative frame.

Add one specific subject, its important story objects, the world’s palette, and the required framing. Supply the Elian portrait as the medium reference, and explicitly distinguish style references from subjects to preserve. Review the new painting beside existing artwork before integrating it.
